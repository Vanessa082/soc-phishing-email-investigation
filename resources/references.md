# References

## Primary source

Bromiley, Matt. *Defend Your Business Against Phishing*. SANS Institute, January 2019.

Relevant sections from the supplied PDF:

- Page 2: Definition of phishing and common desired victim actions.
- Page 3: Sample phishing request for ACH transfer changes.
- Page 4: Explanation of spoofing/look-alike domains, timing, urgency, and fake forwarding.
- Pages 5-8: Technology controls, user training, MFA, link/attachment caution, external-email marking, updates, independent verification, and account auditing.

The screenshots `sans-phishing-email.png` and `sans-phishing-analysis.png` are rendered from pages 3 and 4 of the supplied SANS paper and are included for educational analysis with attribution.

## Project-generated evidence

The Gmail header evidence and screenshots were created through a controlled training email sent between accounts owned by the analyst. Personal addresses and identifiers were sanitized before publication.

The SANS source does not provide raw headers for its phishing example. The repository therefore does not claim authentication or routing results that are absent from the source.

# Legitimate Training Email - Analysis Notes

## Case metadata

- **Case ID:** SOC-2026-0001
- **Analyst:** Wah Vanessa
- **Date:** 31 July 2026
- **Source:** Controlled Gmail-to-Gmail training email
- **Gmail placement:** Spam folder
- **Classification:** Benign training message

## Observations

### Sender and routing

- The visible sender is a Gmail account controlled by the analyst.
- The Return-Path matches the sending Gmail account.
- The message was transferred through Google mail infrastructure.
- The Gmail-generated Message-ID is consistent with a Gmail message.

### Authentication

| Control | Result | Interpretation |
|---|---|---|
| SPF | Pass | The sending Google server was authorized to send for the envelope sender. |
| DKIM | Pass | Gmail's digital signature validated. |
| DMARC | Pass | The visible From domain aligned with an authenticated domain. |

### Content

- The subject contains “Verify Your Account,” a phrase frequently seen in phishing scenarios.
- The body explicitly states that the message is a cybersecurity training exercise.
- No link was included.
- No attachment was included.
- No credential request was made.
- No urgent deadline or threat was included.

### Spam-folder observation

The message appeared in Gmail's Spam folder. Spam placement is a signal generated by the provider, not proof that a message is malicious. The authentication and content evidence must still be reviewed.

## Assessment

The message is a benign training email. It passed SPF, DKIM, and DMARC; its sender information was consistent; and it contained no active phishing mechanism. No indicators of compromise were identified.

## Publication note

Personal addresses and message identifiers were sanitized before being added to the public repository.
